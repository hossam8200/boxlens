Port intelligence

5,410 ports,
every physical characteristic.

The NGA World Port Index (Pub 150) powers real port matching by great-circle distance — channel depth, cargo pier depth, anchorage, tidal range, harbor size, max vessel, and entrance restrictions (swell / ice / tide).

  • Nearby terminals widget — diversion & alternative berths within 60 km, no Overpass dependency
  • Port characteristics — full WPI detail for the representative port
  • UN/LOCODE registry fused with WPI depth soundings

Honesty-first, end to end

No fake numbers.
Ever.

Eight honesty rules are baked into the UI — the disclaimer strip, the VGM DRAFT — NOT CERTIFIED stamp, the arrivals "AIS shows vessels, not containers" rule, and provenance badges on every single number. When a free-tier source is missing, the widget says UNAVAILABLE and explains why — it never invents a figure to look complete.
